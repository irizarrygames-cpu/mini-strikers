Mini Strikers — Privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Mini Strikers is a football game. This page explains, in plain words, what it keeps and what it does not. It is short because the game does not collect much.

If you never make an account

Nothing of yours leaves your device. Your progress — coins, players, what you have won — is stored in your own browser and goes nowhere else. You can play the whole game this way.

If you make an account

Making an account lets you play against other people and keep your progress across devices. To do that, the game stores:

  • The username you chose. Other players can see it during a match and on the leaderboard. Pick a name you are happy for people to see.
  • Your password, scrambled. It is put through PBKDF2 before it is stored, so what is kept cannot be turned back into your password — not by us, not by anybody who got hold of the file.
  • Your game progress. Coins, the country you play for, what you own, your record, your rank, and who your friends in the game are.
  • A note of each coin pack you buy, if you buy any: which pack, the day, and the store's reference number for the purchase. This is so a purchase is never counted twice and never lost. No card number, no billing details.

That is the whole list. No email address, no real name, no age, no phone number, no address, no photographs, and nothing from anywhere else on your device.

Buying coins

In the app, coins can be bought in packs for real money. This happens only through the App Store or Google Play: they take the payment, and the game never sees a card number or any billing detail. Before anything is bought, the game asks a grown-up to type a few numbers that are written out in words. Parents can also use the family controls built into their phone to approve or block purchases.

When you buy a pack, the game sends the store's reference for that purchase to its own server, which checks it with Apple or Google and then adds the coins to your account. Coins cannot be turned back into money. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by the game. Buying coins needs an account, so the coins follow you to any device.

What it does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no third-party trackers of any kind.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anybody.
  • No location, no contacts, no camera, no microphone, no files.

Who can see it

Other players can see your username, the country you play for, your rank and your position on the leaderboard. Nothing else is shown to anyone.

Deleting it

You can delete your account from inside the game: Settings → Delete Account. It asks for your password, and then the account and everything in it is removed from the server, your sessions are ended, and you come off other players' friends lists. It is immediate and it cannot be undone.

If you only want to start again without losing the account, Settings → Reset Save clears your progress and keeps the account.

Where it is kept

Accounts are stored on the game's own server and its database. They are not copied anywhere else.

Who it is for

Mini Strikers is for everyone, of every age. It is also made to be safe for children to play: it asks for no personal information at all beyond a username and a password, usernames are filtered for inappropriate words, and in a match players can only send short ready-made messages, never anything typed. If you are a parent and want an account removed, use Settings → Delete Account in the game, or get in touch at the address below.
